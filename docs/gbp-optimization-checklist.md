## Why this order
Categories + NAP consistency drive *which* searches you appear in. Description, services, and products drive *relevance*. Photos, posts, Q&A, and review responses drive *engagement signals* — Google's tie-breaker between similar shops. Good Tire (your main competitor) is beatable on freshness: most local shops set GBP once and ignore it.
